<p class="sec-label">About</p>

<div class="bio-block">
<p>
I am Aminur Rahman, currently on <b>study leave</b> to pursue a <b>PhD in Information and Computer Science</b> while holding the position of <b>Lecturer</b> in the Department of <a href="https://diu.ac/programs/department-of-cse" style="color:#0049FF;">CSE</a> at <a href="https://diu.ac/" style="color:#0049FF;">DIU</a>. I also serve as a <b>Graduate Teaching Assistant (GTA)</b> at the <a href="https://www.kfupm.edu.sa/departments/ICS/Pages/default.aspx" style="color:#0049FF;">Department of Information and Computer Science</a> at <a href="https://www.kfupm.edu.sa/" style="color:#0049FF;">King Fahd University of Petroleum and Minerals (KFUPM)</a>. I completed my Bachelor's degree from the Department of <a href="https://cuet.ac.bd/dept/cse/" style="color:#0049FF;">CSE</a> at <a href="https://cuet.ac.bd/" style="color:#0049FF;">CUET</a> in 2023.
</p>
<p>
I am deeply passionate about <b>Natural Language Processing (NLP)</b>, <b>Large Language Models (LLMs)</b>, and <b>Neural Networks (NN)</b>. My research focuses on <b>text classification, sentiment analysis, multimodal content analysis</b>, and developing <b>language technologies for e-commerce, healthcare, and social media</b>. I am particularly committed to creating AI-driven solutions for underrepresented languages such as Bengali, bridging the gap between advanced AI technologies and low-resource linguistic communities.
</p>
<p>
As a graduate researcher at <a href="https://kcr-lab.github.io/" style="color:#0049FF;"><b>KCR-Lab</b></a>, I work on cutting-edge projects under the supervision of Prof. Dr. Muhammad Ibrahim Khan, spanning multimodal AI, text-vision analysis, and practical applications of LLMs. At DIU, I serve as the <b>Advisor for the DIU Cyber Security Club</b>, mentoring students, organizing workshops, and facilitating academic activities.
</p>
</div>
